# Senior/Team Lead interview guide

## 30-second answer

The control tower balances service and working capital at product–warehouse–time grain. It keeps current and future facts separate, preserves gross shortage and surplus, maps risk classes to operational playbooks, and moves from enterprise condition to accountable action. The lead challenge is governing a broad measure/visual surface with correctness, performance, security, and clear decision rights.

## Five-minute path

1. Establish grain, time boundary, component eligibility, and cost basis.
2. Walk the decision path from Overview to Network, Surplus, Shortage, and Detail.
3. Explain why gross risk, value, and quantity remain visible.
4. Show risk-to-playbook ownership and Route Checking controls.
5. Treat 355 report objects and a large measure surface as governed risks.
6. Close with release gates, safe AI fallback, incident ownership, and 10× triggers.

## Challenge questions

- Can net risk hide two large exposures?
- How do you prevent on-order and in-transit double counting?
- What is the boundary between current and future facts?
- How do you prove risk buckets are complete and exclusive?
- What happens when AI insight is stale, restricted, or unavailable?
- Which intervention is recommended for surplus versus persistent shortage?

Use observed/inferred/proposed language and do not invent personal scope or business impact.
